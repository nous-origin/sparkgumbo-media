Questions

Is anything a subscription?

No. Every product is a one-time purchase. You pay once and keep the files.

How do I get my files?

Right after checkout you get an email with your download links. Keep it so you can download again any time.

What ages are these for?

Each product lists its own age range. TRUCE works for kids about 6 to 15, and the task ideas are split by age so siblings can use the same system.

What if it doesn't work for us?

Email hello@sparkgumbo.com within 30 days and we'll refund you. See our refund policy.

Do I need special supplies?

A home printer and regular paper. Card stock is nice for agreements and cards but not needed.
